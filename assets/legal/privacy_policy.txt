1. Information Collection and Use

The Application collects certain information to provide and improve our service to you.

A. Personally Identifiable Information You Provide:
For account creation, authentication, and support ticket submission, we may ask you to provide:
* Email Address
* Full Name
* Phone Number
* Other Personal Details (such as address or vehicle registration number) if required for the service request.

B. Payment Facilitation:
The Application displays QR codes to facilitate payments from you to DESKGOO CONSULTING PRIVATE LIMITED for services or products. The Application itself does not process payments or store your payment card/bank details. All payments are handled directly between you and your payment provider/bank when you scan the QR code.

C. User-Provided Media and Content:
To enable you to raise and describe support issues, the Application requests access to:
* Camera: To take new photos related to an issue.
* Photos/Media/Files: To upload existing images or documents from your device.
* Microphone: To record audio notes or descriptions, if this feature is utilized.

Purpose: This media is collected solely for the purpose of documenting and resolving the support ticket you are creating.

D. Automatically Collected Information:
For app functionality and basic analytics, we may automatically collect:
* Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address.
* Device type and operating system version.
* App usage statistics (e.g., feature usage, session length, crash reports).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:
* Creating and managing your user account.
* Authenticating you and providing access to the Application.
* Processing your support requests, including using submitted media to understand and resolve issues.
* Facilitating payments by displaying QR codes (we do not handle the actual payment transaction).
* Communicating with you regarding your account, support tickets, and important updates.
* Improving the Application's performance and user experience through internal analysis of aggregated, non-identifiable data.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

* We do not sell your personal data.

These third parties have access to your information only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

We will retain your User Provided Data (like your account information and support tickets) for as long as you maintain an account with us and as necessary to provide services. Media files attached to closed support tickets may be archived or deleted after a reasonable retention period.

If you wish to delete your account and associated personal data, please contact us at info@telemko.com. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.
